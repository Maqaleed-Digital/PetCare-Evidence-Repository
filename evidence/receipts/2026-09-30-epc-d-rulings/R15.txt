an authorised mechanism.

- live DB apply/mutation;
- real provider activation;
- DNS;

- production secrets;

A destructive or irreversible action without explicit existing authority.

A genuine unresolved A/B changes product/business semantics and existing
authority cannot resolve it.

Do NOT manufacture Sponsor decisions for ordinary implementation choices.
