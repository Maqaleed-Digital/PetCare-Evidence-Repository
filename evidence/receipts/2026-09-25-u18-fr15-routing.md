## Build
- `routing.py`: the ratified OPTIMAL_ROUTING_RULE — (1) licensed for every supply class of the basket, (2) complete
  basket from ledger balances, (3) lowest ETA, (4) distance, (5) stable pharmacy id. `UnavailableMapsAdapter` is the
  served adapter: without a maps provider, routing fails closed (503 ROUTING_UNAVAILABLE, audited) — no hard-coded
  distance, never guessed.
- Migration 0050: pharmacy coordinates on `inventory_location`; `pharmacy_licence` (governed register, no served write
  path — populated by test fixtures only, as the ratified text directs until MVC-PHARM-001 §6c); `routing_decision`
  (owner location, every candidate with its licence/basket/ETA/distance, chosen pharmacy, rule version; non-empty).
- `POST /api/orders/{id}/route` (the order's owner or staff; the session tenant's pharmacies only): decision recorded
  and audited `order.routed`; no qualifying pharmacy → 409 with the recorded decision. `GET …/routing`; the order view
  carries `fulfilled_by`; `/owner/orders` shows the fulfilling pharmacy. A routed order is delivered only from the
  routed pharmacy (409 NOT_THE_ROUTED_PHARMACY).
