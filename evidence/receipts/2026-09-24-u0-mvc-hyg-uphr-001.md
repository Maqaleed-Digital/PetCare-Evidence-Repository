## Defect
`petcare_runtime/src/petcare/uphr/service.py` `get_timeline` (search at the list comprehension, base lines 236-244)
kept an item iff `lowered in str(item).lower()`. Records carry uuid4 identifiers minted at `service.py:74,92,115,134,155,193`,
so a term such as "cbc" matched any record whose id contained it. This flaked
`petcare_runtime/tests/test_ep01_ep02_wave_03.py::test_timeline_can_search` (failed PR #42 CI attempt 1).
