R18.2 — Scope of R17.3 and test entity data: CLARIFIED AND AUTHORIZED.

R17.3 prohibits synthetic, test or non-production DETERMINATIONS: counsel, regulator or other authority rulings asserting that a capability is lawful or authorized, including REG-02.

It does NOT prohibit test entity data that the product itself is required to validate, including licences, registrations and permits.

Where an existing frozen test needs such entity data solely to satisfy a newly enforced real product precondition, a precondition-only fixture amendment is authorized provided:
- the fixture represents entity data, not a regulatory/counsel determination;
- production validation is exercised rather than bypassed;
- existing assertion semantics remain unchanged;
- every existing assertion remains byte-identical / AST-equivalent;
- before/after hashes and the fixture change are recorded.

No synthetic determination may be introduced under this ruling.

R18.3 — Establishment-licence enforcement for the J-O8 order path: REQUIRED; create a new CP1 row.

First identify from the authoritative FR-20/J-O8 domain model which regulated establishment participates in the order fulfilment/dispensing path. Do not invent or change the actor relationship.

Before the regulated fulfilment/dispensing action is accepted, the served product must verify that the applicable establishment has a valid licence on the Asia/Riyadh calendar date.

An absent, expired, wrong-tenant or otherwise invalid applicable establishment licence must cause refusal, and the refusal must be audited.

Frozen FR-20 tests may receive only the precondition entity-data fixtures authorized by R18.2. Their existing assertions must remain byte-identical / AST-equivalent.

Required evidence includes positive, absent, expired, wrong-tenant and boundary-date cases plus an ARMED perturbation removing/bypassing the licence control.

Implement this control in D2h before J-O8 is counted complete.

If the authoritative FR-20/J-O8 model does not identify an applicable regulated establishment for this action, do not invent one: ledger that exact modelling gap and STOP only this journey pending Sponsor disposition; continue other unblocked work under R18.4.
