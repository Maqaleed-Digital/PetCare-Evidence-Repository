## Defect found and fixed
Creating and signing consultation notes and recording medical history required only the veterinarian ROLE; an
unverified (or lapsed) veterinarian could sign a clinical record — exactly AC-FR-05-01's fails_if. All three now
require the live practitioner authority, and every refusal is audited (`practitioner.authority.refused`).

## Build
- Registration (`routers/auth.py`): a veterinarian must supply `licence {licence_number, issuing_authority,
  expires_on}`; missing/invalid -> 400 LICENCE_DETAILS_REQUIRED, expired -> 400 LICENCE_EXPIRED, both BEFORE the
  invite is spent. The licence is stored (immutable, SUBMITTED). Owners are unaffected.
- Migration 0043: `vet_licence`, `vet_licence_verification` (UNIQUE per licence; CHECK verifier and basis named).
- Admin (platform_admin, session tenant): `GET /api/admin/practitioners/licences`, `POST …/licences/{id}/verify`
  — MANUAL_STAFF must state what the licence was checked against; AUTHORITY_LOOKUP asks the port and accepts only an
  explicit VALID (served adapter: UNAVAILABLE). Never self-verified; never twice; an expired licence cannot be
  verified. Verification mints the U5 practitioner grant with `expires_at` = end of the licence's expiry day, so the
  expiry is re-checked at every clinical act. Audited `practitioner.licence.verified` with method and grant.
- `GET /api/practitioners/me/licence`. UI: `/register` asks a veterinarian for licence details (Arabic default).
