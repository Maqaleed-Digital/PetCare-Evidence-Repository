## What is built
- `reminders.vaccination_card(dues, completion_of, name_of)`: **GIVEN** = a VACCINATION item with a RECORDED completion (title, the
  Asia/Riyadh date of the completion, the veterinarian who recorded it); **DUE** = a VACCINATION item with no completion (title,
  due date). A TREATMENT never appears; an overdue item stays DUE — nothing is inferred from a due date.
- `ReminderRepository.completion()` (in-memory + PostgreSQL, over the existing `pet_care_completion`, migration 0048; no migration).
- Served route `GET /api/pets/{pet_id}/vaccination-card` (PET_READERS; scoped by `_pet_or_404`: another owner's or another
  tenant's pet is 404). `requirements/served_routes.json` regenerated by its tool (133 -> 134 routes); `status.json` changes only in
  `served_route_count` — no FR state changes.
- Web: `/owner/pets/[petId]/vaccination-card` (ar default RTL / en), linked from the pet profile (`pet-vaccination-card`).
  **PDF**: the browser's own print engine ("Save as PDF" via `window.print()`); print styles keep the card and drop the site
  chrome and actions. No PDF library, service or provider is involved, and none is simulated. The e2e proves a PDF is produced
  from the page (Chromium `page.pdf`, `%PDF-` header) with the actions hidden in print media.
