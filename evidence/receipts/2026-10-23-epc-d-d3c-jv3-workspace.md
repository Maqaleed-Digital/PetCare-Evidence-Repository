## What is built
- `soap_notes.py`: the consultation's OWN veterinarian writes a clinical note in four SOAP sections. Each save is a new
  insert-only VERSION; the latest is the draft in force. A draft is **UNSIGNED** — nothing here signs, locks or finalises a note.
  Validation: at least one written section (whitespace is not a note), each section <= 4000 characters, a known template or none.
- Templates — **assumption recorded (D3C-SOAP-TEMPLATES-STRUCTURAL):** two templates (General consultation, Follow-up visit),
  Arabic and English, each section is headings only (every line ends in ":"); no finding, diagnosis, dose or clinical value. The
  screen applies a template to EMPTY sections only — what the veterinarian wrote is never replaced.
- **Assumption recorded (D3C-DRAFT-OPEN-UNTIL-SIGNED):** a draft stays editable after the outcome is recorded; the point at which a
  note becomes immutable is the signature, which is J-V4.
- Migration **0066** (additive): `consultation_soap_draft` (FK consultation, tenant, user_identity; section length CHECKs; a CHECK
  that refuses an all-blank draft). `PostgresSoapDraftRepository` + in-memory twin; `pg_harness` truncates it before
  `consultation`.
- Served routes (+2, `served_routes.json` regenerated by its tool, 145 -> 147; `status.json` changes only `served_route_count`;
  no FR state change):
  - `GET /api/vet/consultations/{id}/workspace` — veterinarian only (owner 403); another veterinarian's or another tenant's
    consultation is 404 (never confirmed); returns the consultation, patient (name, species, breed), owner name, the draft in
    force, the version count, the templates and the REG-02 gate. Audited `consultation.workspace.viewed`.
  - `PUT /api/vet/consultations/{id}/soap` — same ownership rule, plus a practitioner authority in force (FR-05) at the act; a new
    version; audited `consultation.soap_draft.saved` with the template id only — the note's text never enters the audit.
- Web: `/vet/consultations/[sessionId]` (CV-05, ar default RTL / en), reached from `/vet/queue` (`queue-open-workspace` on each
  open consultation; the D3b lines of the queue are unchanged). The workspace also records the visit outcome through the existing
  FR-06 route, after which the consultation leaves the queue. A remote consultation shows the REG-02 notice and no video control.
  No signing control exists on the page (asserted in vitest and e2e).

## Findings
- **D3C-BOOKING-TO-CONSULTATION-NO-SCREEN**: no served screen turns a booked appointment (CV-03) into a consultation; the spec
  starts the consultation through the veterinarian's API session (as CO-10 did). Booking and consultation carry no link. Left to

  reconcile it with the durable SOAP draft before signing can count. Candidate ledger row — not written (no Notion this session).
