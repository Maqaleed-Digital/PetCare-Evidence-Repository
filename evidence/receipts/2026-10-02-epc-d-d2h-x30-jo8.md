## R18.3 — the applicable establishment (domain model, not invented)
The regulated fulfilment/dispensing action is `POST /api/orders/{order_id}/deliver`: stock leaves as SUPPLY movements
(`StockMovement(reason=SUPPLY)`) from `body.location_id`, and FR-15 already constrains that location to be "the routed
pharmacy" (`NOT_THE_ROUTED_PHARMACY`). The supplying location is therefore the regulated establishment; no actor relationship was
added or changed. Before D2h no dated licence existed for it (FR-15's register is `(location_id, supply_class)` booleans, no
dates, no served write path — "until MVC-PHARM-001 §6c defines establishment licences").
